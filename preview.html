04 / PETSCII front legendsView reference sheet ↗

All 63 graphic legends, reconstructed as smooth outlines with their cell frames. On each key: Commodore on the left, Shift on the right. Click a symbol to insert it above.

Labels show PETSCII bytes in hexadecimal. These describe C64 uppercase/graphics mode; the font uses separate private-use characters. Shapes and mappings are documented; print weights and proportions are inferred. Pi also works with Commodore + up-arrow.
